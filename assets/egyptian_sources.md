## Selected concept

**CONCEPT 1 — CAIRO NIGHT** (`cairo_night.jpg`) is the default background.

Rationale (per the Cinematic Intro 2.0 brief):
- Wide 1920×966 frame with generous **negative space** in the upper region where the brand lives.
- City skyline at night reads as **modern Egyptian technology**, not tourism.
- Night + city lights pair naturally with the DelwaQty purple/blue/cyan palette.
- No pyramids in the foreground → the logo stays the hero.

The other assets are retained as packaged alternates so a designer can switch the
concept without a rebuild of sources.
